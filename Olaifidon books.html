About Me

Abimbola (Ola) Ifidon

Author ? Speaker ? Business & Career Strategist ? Executive Managing Director, ValCon Inc.

Abimbola (Ola) Ifidon is the first daughter of six children born to the late Johnson Arowosegbe and Veronica Arowosegbe, both of whom were astute scholars with a deep commitment to education and intellectual excellence.

She is an accomplished professional with extensive expertise and a distinguished academic background. She holds dual Master of Business Administration (MBA) degrees from Cornell University, USA, and Queen's University, Canada, and has earned a merit award in Economics. She was also the first Certified Business Analysis Professional (CBAP) in Alberta, Canada. Demonstrating resilience and determination, Ola earned her Chartered Director (C.Dir) designation from McMaster University in Ontario while navigating a particularly challenging period in her life, further solidifying her reputation as a purpose-driven transformational leader.

Having faced her share of life's challenges?from overcoming a major health crisis, as chronicled in her book Rescued, to navigating job loss and toxic environments?Ola has continuously redefined herself. Drawing on inner strength and a resilient spirit, she has used adversity as a catalyst for growth, transforming setbacks into stepping stones for success.

Passionate about empowering others, Ola is the Executive Managing Director, Strategy & Business Solutions at ValCon Inc., an Alberta-based management firm. A business and career strategist, sought-after speaker, and author of several books, including Rescued and SELL Me: Strategies for Crafting an Impactful Resume.

In Recalibrate, she shares practical strategies for not just bouncing back from life's setbacks but springing forward?reframing limitations as opportunities for growth and redefining success on one's own terms.

Ola is happily married to her college sweetheart, Kenneth Ifidon, and together they are raising three remarkable sons.

The Rescue Story

Rescued

A memoir of faith and fate

Life happens to us all. One day we are on the mountain of hope and optimism; the next we are in the valley of hopelessness and despair. When you receive that devastating message from your spouse, your banker, or your doctor, do not give up?all hope is not lost.

“Rescued” is a story of courage and resilience in the face of difficult challenges. It offers hope and strategies on how to navigate the turbulent times of life.

Rescued is an odyssey that goes beyond information sharing. It is packed with insights, revelations and strategies that will inspire you to hope again.

I did not start off thinking I would be writing a book. I was only journaling my experience as part of a therapeutic mental exercise. No sooner had I started than I began to realize how my experience would benefit people going through similar or more challenging situations. We all get used to having a full cup, but at some point life will hand you a half-filled cup?you may choose to see it as half-full or half-empty; your perspective will largely determine your attitude, actions, and the strength you generate from within.

As a recipient of grace, I wanted to give back?to share my struggles, vulnerabilities, and successes, as well as the strategies that helped me navigate the turbulence, and to proclaim the faithfulness of God.

Narrative Nonfiction

On the Other Side of the Night

When the Lights Go Out: Cross the Night

On the Other Side of the Night is a deeply reflective narrative nonfiction work that explores fear, faith, leadership, and the quiet courage required when life interrupts certainty. It is about decision-making under terror, trusting life again, and discovering peace when nothing feels safe.

It is about standing at the intersection of knowledge and emotion, where logic cannot quiet the heart, and courage must be summoned without guarantees. This story explores what it means to choose peace without knowing the outcome, to trust life when control slips away, and to move forward when every instinct wants to freeze. It is about the unseen battles?decision fatigue, anticipatory grief, identity disruption, and the quiet reckoning with one’s own mortality.

This is a story of agency reclaimed, of fear faced?not conquered, but walked through. It is about surrender that is not defeat, and faith that shows up as steadiness rather than certainty. Ultimately, this book is an offering of hope?for anyone navigating diagnosis or divorce, loss or leadership, illness or irreversible change.

This is not a story of survival alone. It is a story of becoming.
