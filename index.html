About Me

Abimbola (Ola) Ifidon

Author • Speaker • Business & Career Strategist • Executive Managing Director, ValCon Inc.

Abimbola (Ola) Ifidon is the first daughter of six children born to the late Johnson Arowosegbe and Veronica Arowosegbe, both of whom were astute scholars with a deep commitment to education and intellectual excellence.

She is an accomplished professional with extensive expertise and a distinguished academic background. She holds dual Master of Business Administration (MBA) degrees from Cornell University, USA, and Queen's University, Canada, and has earned a merit award in Economics. She was also the first Certified Business Analysis Professional (CBAP) in Alberta, Canada. Demonstrating resilience and determination, Ola earned her Chartered Director (C.Dir) designation from McMaster University in Ontario while navigating a particularly challenging period in her life, further solidifying her reputation as a purpose-driven transformational leader.

Having faced her share of life's challenges—from overcoming a major health crisis, as chronicled in her book Rescued, to navigating job loss and toxic environments—Ola has continuously redefined herself. Drawing on inner strength and a resilient spirit, she has used adversity as a catalyst for growth, transforming setbacks into stepping stones for success.

Passionate about empowering others, Ola is the Executive Managing Director, Strategy & Business Solutions at ValCon Inc., an Alberta-based management firm. A business and career strategist, sought-after speaker, and author of several books, including Rescued and SELL Me: Strategies for Crafting an Impactful Resume.

In Recalibrate, she shares practical strategies for not just bouncing back from life's setbacks but springing forward—reframing limitations as opportunities for growth and redefining success on one's own terms.

Ola is happily married to her college sweetheart, Kenneth Ifidon, and together they are raising three remarkable sons.
